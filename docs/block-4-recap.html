Slide 1 / 1 · Q&A

Questions?

15 min · the floor

Martin Rojas + Samuel Obukwelu · workshop materials at github.com/<repo-url> · feedback welcome

Speaker notes

Open the floor. "Fifteen minutes. Any question — Block 1, 2, 3, or 4. Bring your own use case if you want to apply this to something specific."

Field questions in priority order:

  1. Tactical "how do I do X with our stack?" — most actionable, most useful to room
  2. Strategic "should we adopt this pattern?" — invite the room into the discussion
  3. Conceptual "why this design over that?" — give your real opinion, with the trade-off

If the room is quiet — seed a question: "Common one: how do you decide between Bifrost OSS plus app-layer guardrails versus Bifrost enterprise tier with built-in guardrails? Answer is — if you're small, OSS plus app-layer. If you're regulated, enterprise. If you're somewhere in between, run OSS until the audit pressure forces enterprise. Don't over-buy on day one."

Final 30 seconds: thank the room. Point at the workshop repo. Invite follow-up over LinkedIn / Twitter / however you prefer.
